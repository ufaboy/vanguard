Before you deploy

Begin with First Contact (20 hostiles), then unlock Broken Avenue, Civic Shelter, and Railway Cut. On defense missions, protect the allied Command Base HQ as well as your tank.

Survive and recover

Use cover, attack exposed flanks, and shoot wooden BOX caches to release Power-Ups. Your field base repairs damage while it remains intact. Below 33% hull integrity, movement speed drops by 30%.

Build your tank

Visit the Hangar & Workshop to upgrade all five core systems across five tiers and equip standalone modules. Open the 3D Codex to inspect enemy armor and weapons.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do tank stats and armor penetration work?

Every tank has a base hull plus 5 core tracks (Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine, Armor) and optional modules. Armor is calculated per hit side (front, left, right, rear). Cannon penetration reduces the target’s effective armor percentage before final damage is applied.

How do I cross water or drive smoothly on ice?

Upgrade your Chassis to Tier IV (All-Terrain Tracks) or Tier V (Grav-Hover). Both cross deep water and handle ice, mud, and sand without slip or slowdown.

How does the Active Protection System (APS) work?

The 4-corner APS intercepts incoming Cannon Mk1–Mk3 shells and missiles for 1 charge each. Heavy Cannon Mk4/Mk5, Howitzer, and Mammoth Dual shells cost 2 charges. Flamethrowers and Titan Rail shots bypass APS.

How are credits, repairs, and progress saved?

Your campaign unlocks, credits, component tiers, and survival records are stored locally in your browser via localStorage and persist across all pages and sessions.

What happens if I abort a mission early?

Collected credits and salvaged loot remain in your save, while uncollected battlefield drops are lost.
