Iron Crucible — ENDLESS ARMORED HOLDOUT

Hold a 36×36 fortified industrial crossroads against escalating combined-arms waves. All enemy vehicles use their authentic catalog specifications — threat scales through squad size, composition, and multi-angle pressure.

1. Isolated Tier I Session Progression
Every run starts fresh at Tier I across all 5 tracks (Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine, Armor) with $500. Earn session credits from kills and wave clear bonuses to upgrade at the Allied Base Hangar [U]. Main profile credits and upgrades are untouched.
2. Allied HQ & Field Recovery
Your Command Base repairs hull and subsystems and unlocks Hangar [U]. If the HQ is destroyed, the battle continues — break newly dropped supply crates (BOXES) to recover a BASE_RESTORE pickup and bring the workshop back online.
3. Intermission & Early Wave Call [N]
Clearing a wave grants a 15-second tactical intermission and drops fresh supply crates and explosive barrels. Press [N] (or the HUD button) during intermission to call the next wave immediately for a +$150 readiness bonus.
