Survival Mode Briefing: Paris — Last Stand

Survival is the base defense holdout of BATTLE TANK, a free 3D isometric tank game, set in fortified central Paris. You deploy on a stock Tier I VANGUARD tank with $500 in session credits and one job: keep the 120 HP Élysée Palace HQ standing for seven minutes while armored columns push in from every side of the city. Hold the line long enough and you can extract with a victory — or stay for endless Overtime and chase a higher record.

How a Survival run works

Waves arrive on a strict 60-second clock at 0:00, 1:00, 2:00 and onward, whether or not you have finished the previous attackers. Falling behind means fighting two waves at once, so speed matters as much as accuracy. Between waves there is a short intermission; calling the next wave early pays a +$150 bonus.

Win and loss conditions

Reach 7:00 with the Élysée Palace HQ intact to win. The run ends in defeat if the HQ falls or your own tank is destroyed. After the win you choose: extract and bank the result, or continue into Overtime.

Credits and the field Garage

Each cleared wave pays $250 plus $50 per wave number, and boss waves add a $600 bonus. Spend it in the field Garage [U] near your HQ to upgrade Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine and Armor during the run. Survival credits never carry over to your Campaign or Contracts profile, so every run starts on an even footing.

The seven-minute wave schedule

  1. 0:00 — Recon Probe. Scouts, line tanks and a mine-laying sapper test your perimeter.
  2. 1:00 — Boulevard Bridgehead. Line tanks and heavy armor push down the avenues with an engineer welding them back together.
  3. 2:00 — Seine River Raiders. Hover tanks strike from the river under howitzer cover.
  4. 3:00 — Champs-Élysées Breachers. Twin heavy tanks, a kamikaze rammer and missile strikers try to break the walls.
  5. 4:00 — Electronic Siege. A jammer, a stealth sniper and artillery blind and shell the defense.
  6. 5:00 — Grand Pincer. Every enemy role at once: armor, flamethrowers, kamikazes, strikers and howitzers.
  7. 6:00 — Finale. The MAMMOTH MK-V siege dreadnought arrives with an armored escort.

Overtime, bosses and the Paris battlefield

Endless Overtime

After 7:00 the waves keep coming and grow larger, faster and better coordinated. Every fifth wave (10, 15, 20, 25 …) is a boss wave that rotates through HYDRA MLRS-X, CERBERUS-T and the SUPREME DREADNOUGHT before the cycle repeats at a higher tier.

A fortified city

The map is a 40×40 destructible slice of Paris with the Eiffel Tower, Arc de Triomphe, Louvre Pyramid, Notre-Dame, Sacré-Cœur and the Concorde obelisk. Minefields, concrete barricades and brick walls shape the attack lanes, and you can blast new ones.

Supplies on every wave

Each wave drops three supply crates and two explosive barrels onto the map. Shoot crates open for credits and field power-ups such as Repair, EMP and APS charges, and lure enemy columns past barrels before you detonate them.

Records worth chasing

Your best wave, most kills, longest survival time and number of Paris victories are saved in your browser and shown on this screen, so every run is a chance to beat your own score.
