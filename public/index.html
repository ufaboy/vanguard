About BATTLE TANK

A free tank game you play in the browser

BATTLE TANK is a free 3D tank shooter that runs directly in your web browser through WebGL. There is nothing to download or install and no account to create: open the page, pick a mode and you are on the battlefield in seconds. It is built for desktop computers and tablets in landscape orientation, with keyboard and mouse controls — WASD to drive, the mouse to aim the turret and the left button to fire.

Classic arcade roots, modern tactics

The game takes the top-down tank battles of the arcade era and rebuilds them as an isometric 3D tactics game. Every arena is a 40×40 grid where walls crumble under fire: brick breaks block by block, reinforced concrete cracks and exposes its rebar, and forests hide tanks from view. Armor is calculated per side, so hitting an enemy in the flank or rear matters far more than firing straight into its front plate.

Build your own tank

Your VANGUARD tank is assembled from five upgradeable components — Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine and Armor — each with five tiers you can see change on the 3D model. Earn credits in battle, spend them in the Garage and add standalone modules such as the 4-corner Active Protection System, a homing ATGM missile pod or an emergency repair kit.

Enemies that think

Twelve enemy tank classes fight with their own roles: scouts flank, line tanks peek from cover, howitzers shell you from hiding, sappers lay mines on your route and stealth snipers vanish after each shot. A squad director limits how many shoot at once and spreads them around you, so every fight feels like a coordinated assault rather than a crowd.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Campaign unlocks, credits, component tiers, medals and Survival records are stored in your browser, so you can close the tab and continue later on the same device.
