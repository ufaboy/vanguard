Privacy Policy

BATTLE TANK is designed to run directly in your web browser without player accounts, logins, or personal profile collection.

1. Local Browser Storage

To save your game progress and preferences between sessions, BATTLE TANK stores data locally on your device using browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us:

  • Campaign mission unlocks, contract completions, credits, and survival high scores.
  • Tank loadout, 5-track component upgrade tiers, and equipped optional modules (iron_vanguard_upgrades_v3 and save repository keys).
  • Audio, camera preset, graphics quality, and FPS cap preferences (iron_vanguard_settings_v1).

You can delete all locally saved data at any time by clearing site data in your browser settings.

2. Server & Hosting Logs

BATTLE TANK is served as a static web application. Like standard web hosting providers, our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare Pages) may automatically process basic technical request metadata—such as IP address, browser user-agent, requested URL, and timestamp—for security, DDoS protection, and reliable delivery.

3. Advertising, Cookies & Third-Party Vendors

BATTLE TANK uses Google AdSense and Google H5 Games Ads to display advertisements (such as optional rewarded video ads and between-mission ad breaks). Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or local device identifiers to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to BATTLE TANK and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn more about how Google processes data on partner sites in Google’s Privacy & Terms.

Pages may also link to external platforms such as GitHub for source code or issue reporting; any external website you visit is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under 13, because the game operates without accounts, forms, or personal data collection.

5. Policy Updates & Contact

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if online leaderboards or optional analytics are introduced), this page will be updated accordingly. For any privacy questions, please visit our Contact page.
