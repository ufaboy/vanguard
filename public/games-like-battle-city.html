Games Like Battle City: BATTLE TANK in 3D

If you grew up with the 1985 arcade tank classic Battle City, also known as Tank 1990, you remember the rules: protect your base, break through brick walls and stop wave after wave of enemy tanks. BATTLE TANK is a free 3D isometric tank game built on that same base-defense idea, redesigned as a modern tactics game that runs in your browser with no download.

What Carries Over From the Classic

The core loop will feel familiar within the first minute. You start next to your own headquarters, enemies pour in from the far side of the map, and the walls around you are both your shield and your obstacle.

BASE DEFENSE Protect Your HQ

Your Command Base sits on the edge of the map behind a concrete line. If enemies destroy it, you lose your repair point, and in base-defense missions you lose the mission.

BRICK & STEEL Walls You Can Shoot Through

Brick walls crumble block by block, while reinforced concrete takes many hits. Opening the right gap at the right time is still half the strategy.

FOREST, WATER, ICE The Classic Terrain Set

Forests hide tanks, deep water blocks standard tracks and ice makes you slide. Each one works the way veterans expect, with extra depth on top.

35 MISSIONS A Full Campaign

The campaign runs for 35 missions across 7 regions, from the city outskirts to the enemy capital, with Sector Bosses guarding the end of each region.

What Is New in BATTLE TANK

BATTLE TANK keeps the spirit of the classic but plays as a tactical game. You see the battlefield in 3D from an isometric camera, aim the turret with the mouse independently from the hull, and plan every engagement around armor angles and cover.

3D ISOMETRIC Real 3D Arenas

Every 40×40 map is rendered in 3D with Three.js and WebGL: walls crack, buildings collapse, forests burn and explosions leave craters.

DIRECTIONAL ARMOR Angles Matter

Tanks have separate front, side and rear armor. Flanking a heavy enemy and hitting its rear can turn a hopeless fight around. Read more in the How to Play guide.

5×5 UPGRADES Build Your Tank

Upgrade Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine and Armor through five tiers each, and add modules like an active protection system or a homing missile pod. See the Tank Arsenal.

SMART ENEMIES 12 Enemy Classes and 4 Bosses

Scouts flank, howitzers shell you from cover, snipers hide and sappers mine your route, while four multi-phase Sector Bosses close the regions. Meet them in the Enemy Guide.

Classic vs. BATTLE TANK at a Glance

Camera and graphics

The classic is a flat top-down 2D game. BATTLE TANK is a 3D isometric game with dynamic lighting, particle effects and detailed tank models.

Controls

The classic uses a D-pad and one fire button. BATTLE TANK uses WASD or arrow keys to drive, the mouse to aim the turret, and extra keys for missiles, the Garage and the tactical map.

Progression

The classic resets your tank between lives. In BATTLE TANK you earn credits, buy permanent upgrades in the Garage, and every upgrade is visible on the 3D model.

Game modes

Beyond the 35-mission campaign, BATTLE TANK has an endless Survival mode and special-operations Contracts such as sabotage and cargo escort missions.

Players

BATTLE TANK is a single-player game focused on tactics against AI opponents. There is no online multiplayer.

How to Start Playing

BATTLE TANK runs in any current desktop browser with WebGL (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari) and on tablets in landscape orientation. Open the Campaign, pick the first mission and you are in the fight within seconds. No download, no installation and no sign-up.
