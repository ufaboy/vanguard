Enemy Tanks & Sector Bosses

Tactical intelligence on all 12 hostile tank classes and 4 Sector Bosses in BATTLE TANK. Every enemy runs a dedicated role brain, directional armor model, and squad coordination director.

4 Sector Bosses

BOSS-01 // SECTOR 01 DREADNOUGHT MAMMOTH MK-V

Super-heavy siege brawler armed with twin BOSS_DUAL cannons, a turret Homing ATGM pod, and onboard APS against player missiles. At 50% HP it activates a 2s phase shield, enters citadel overdrive, and calls an armored escort.

Counter: Bait its ATGM salvo or strip onboard APS charges, then flank its slower hull traverse.

BOSS-02 // AMPHIBIOUS ARTILLERY CRUISER HYDRA MLRS-9

Stand-off rocket cruiser operating at 10–14 cells with telegraphed 3-rocket MLRS triangle salvos, amphibious water crossing, and a close-range flamethrower plus homing ATGM defense inside 6 cells. At 50% HP it overclocks its surviving heads.

Counter: Step out of ground salvo markers, watch the close-range flame arc, and use Tier IV/V chassis on water maps.

BOSS-03 // TWIN-TURRET FORTRESS SUPREME DREADNOUGHT (TITAN)

Guarded by directional Aegis armor (60% front, 35% sides, 15% rear) and two independent dual-cannon turrets. At 60% HP it summons a Standard + Armor escort; at 30% HP it overloads—firing faster while rear radiator grilles open (0% rear armor).

Counter: Never trade shots into its front glacis. Flank behind cover and punish the rear during overload.

BOSS-04 // THERMOBARIC LEVIATHAN CERBERUS-T (INFERNO)

Siege leviathan equipped with a 240mm thermobaric cannon (2.5-cell blast that vaporizes brick, concrete, forest, ice, and water into sand), demolition dozer blade, rear mine dispenser, and nitro overdrive ram.

Counter: Keep distance from walls during thermobaric blasts, watch for rear mines, and dodge its linear overdrive ram.

12 Enemy Tank Classes

LINE INFANTRY // BALANCED Standard

Core battle tank that peeks from firing lanes, ducks into cover after engagements, and retreats once below 35% HP.

FLANKER // HIGH MOBILITY Scout

Fast light tank that circles 6–10 cells toward your rear arc, kites when crowded, and evades after taking hits.

SPEARHEAD // HEAVY ARMOR Armor

Heavy glacis-first tank (40% front / 20% sides / 5% rear) that closes to 2–4 cells. Flank or use high-penetration cannons.

ARTILLERY // INDIRECT FIRE Howitzer

Emplaces hidden behind walls 7–11 cells away and shells your position using allied spotter data. Push inside 5 cells to force relocation.

MINELAYER // AREA DENIAL Sapper

Plants magnetic proximity mines in chokepoints along your recent trail before switching to line combat.

RAIDER // AMPHIBIOUS Hover

Grav-hover unit that strikes from deep water and shorelines impassable to standard Tier I–III chassis.

HUNTER // CONTACT WARHEAD Kamikaze

Unarmed stalker that approaches out of sight, then triggers a telegraphed red-spark zigzag charge inside 5 cells. Destroy before contact.

AMBUSHER // CLOSE QUARTERS Flamethrower

Lurks in forests and corners 3–6 cells away and rushes inside 5 cells. Napalm streams bypass APS completely.

EWAR // ELECTRONIC WARFARE Jammer

Shadows out of sight and dashes in to emit an ECM pulse that disrupts APS radar and missile guidance while allies fight.

SUPPORT // FIELD REPAIR Engineer

Welds damaged enemy tanks, repairs broken brick fortifications, and erects new brick walls. Prioritize in prolonged fights.

STANDOFF // MISSILE CARRIER Striker

Holds 7–10 cell line-of-sight lanes to launch guided missiles, shifting sideways after each salvo. Counter with APS or smoke.

SNIPER // CAMOUFLAGE Stealth

Overwatches from 8–11 cells with full camouflage while stationary, firing a single high-velocity shot before slipping into cover.
