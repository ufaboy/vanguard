CONTRACT-01 // LOGISTICS RAID // RULES OF ENGAGEMENT
1. Explicit Cargo Loading [E]
Stop within 1.5 cells of a sealed data capsule and press [E] (or the Cargo button) for 2.0 seconds. Movement or leaving range cancels loading; enemy hits do not.
2. Southern Delivery Pad Unloading
Drive onto the southern delivery pad (rows 30–32, cols 16–19) and hold position for 2.0 seconds. Movement pauses unloading; leaving the pad resets progress. Each delivered container immediately secures +$1,500.
3. Deliberate Extraction [E]
After 2 deliveries, the Extract [E] control unlocks on the pad (3.0s countdown). Leaving the pad cancels extraction; taking damage or fighting on the pad does not. Extracting at 2/3 pays $6,000 total; delivering all 3 and extracting pays $10,000 total.
