Changelog & Patch Notes

Release history, balance tuning, and new tactical features for BATTLE TANK.

VERSION 1.23.3 // BURNING FOREST ENEMY KILL FIX Fix Enemy Tank Freezing & Becoming Invulnerable When Killed by Burning Forest

Fixed a bug in ForestFireController.update where direct subtraction from enemy.hp before calling damageEnemy caused enemies killed by burning forest to hit the enemy.hp <= DURABILITY.ZERO_HP early return in damageEnemy, leaving an immobile, invulnerable wreck on the burned-out SAND tile.

VERSION 1.23.2 // ENEMY HQ SIEGE RESUPPLY AIR DROP Automatic Air Drop Near Enemy HQ After All Enemy Tanks Are Cleared

On maps with a fortified Enemy Base (ENEMY_BASE), eliminating all enemy tanks while the Enemy HQ is still standing now automatically dispatches a parachute resupply Air Drop onto a passable cell 2–6 tiles from the Enemy HQ so the player can finish demolishing the stronghold without driving back to the Allied Base.

VERSION 1.23.1 // 3D CODEX BUILDING & RUBBLE INSPECTOR 1-Story & 2-Story Modular Buildings & Rubble Added to 3D Codex

Added HOUSE_1 (1-Story Modular Building), HOUSE_2 (2-Story Modular Building), and RUBBLE (Concrete Building Rubble) to SURFACE_CATALOG and the 3D Codex studio. Includes an interactive Building Configurator to inspect all 8 residential styles (Iteration 1), all 4 industrial/warehouse/office styles (Iteration 2), and every progressive destruction stage down to collapsed rubble, plus live SPACE / Test Fire demolition effects.

VERSION 1.23.0 // INDUSTRIAL, WAREHOUSE & OFFICE BUILDINGS ACROSS ALL THEMES New 1–2 Story Industrial, Warehouse & Office Buildings & Theme-Based Map Distribution

Expanded the modular building system (public/models/terrain/buildings.glb) with 4 new 1-story and 4 new 2-story industrial, warehouse, and office building styles (windowless corrugated steel warehouses, sawtooth brick factories, barrel-arch supply depots, heavy industrial plants, and high-bay logistics hubs alongside windowed site, curtain-wall, and administrative offices). Added buildings across all sector themes with theme-specific residential vs. industrial/office proportions: forest (50%/50%), desert (25%/75%), coast (75%/25%), alpine (50%/50%), industrial (25%/75%), and capital (75%/25%).

VERSION 1.22.5 // CONCRETE DAMAGE TINT FIX Darkened Concrete Damage Ramp Matching Intact Concrete Tint

Aligned the per-hit damage darkening ramp in updateConcreteBlockDamage and procedural concrete materials with the intact CONCRETE base color (0x8c96a3) so damaged concrete blocks progressively darken on hit instead of jumping to a legacy lighter shade.

VERSION 1.22.4 // ENEMY UPDATE LOOP CRASH FIX & AUDIO AUTOPLAY GUARD Multi-Kill Enemy Iteration Fix & User-Gesture AudioContext Unlock

Fixed a runtime freeze in EnemyAiController.updateEnemies and explosion/hazard damage loops when a barrel detonation or contact warhead eliminated multiple enemies during a single iteration. Deferred AudioContext initialization until the first user gesture on the page to prevent browser autoplay console warnings while automatically starting the diesel engine loop on first input.

VERSION 1.22.3 // CH4 & CH5 WATER TRAVERSAL BALANCE CH4 Water Speed Penalty & CH5 Full-Speed Amphibious Hover

Differentiated amphibious chassis water traversal: CH4 Active All-Terrain Tracks now crosses deep WATER at 60% speed while retaining full-speed traction across ice, sand, swamp, craters, and oil slicks, whereas CH5 Grav-Hover Hybrid glides across water at full speed with zero penalty.

VERSION 1.22.2 // CONTINUOUS BASE REPAIR & REARM IN HANGAR Base Repair & Ammo Rearming Continue Inside Hangar

Allied base hull, subsystem, and armor panel repairs as well as cannon shell rearming (up to BASE_REARM_CAP) now continue at their normal pace while the player is inside the base hangar, keeping combat units and projectiles paused.

VERSION 1.22.1 // COMBAT HUD READABILITY & URBAN BLOCKS Larger Tactical HUD Panels & Split Oversized City Buildings

Redesigned the top-left sector telemetry card (HOSTILES, CREDITS, CITADEL) and bottom HUD panels (HULL HP, weapon reload, and uniform REPAIR / SMOKE / ATGM / APS module tiles) with larger typography, progress bars, and high-contrast pure-CSS state styling. Rewrote 22 campaign, contract, and survival maps to split 143 oversized building clusters into smaller houses (≤ 3×3) separated by 1-cell alleys.

VERSION 1.22.0 // HYBRID 1–2 STORY BUILDINGS, RUBBLE & LOFTED MISSILES Modular 1–2 Story City Buildings, Persistent Rubble & Top-Attack Missiles

Added a Blender-built modular building system (public/models/terrain/buildings.glb) with 8 1-story and 8 2-story facade styles sharing a single 1024×1024 atlas and ORM map; 2×2+ BRICK and CONCRETE clusters form cohesive multi-tile buildings with 5 (1-story) and 7 (2-story) destruction stages while 1-tile walls remain classic blocks. Destroyed buildings leave persistent RUBBLE that slows all non-boss tanks until cleared by DOZER_BLADE or heavy HE blasts, and player ATGMs now climb above rooftops before diving steeply onto targets.

Increased destructible supply crate (BOXES) count in Mission 35 (Vanguard Dawn) by over 30% (from 8 to 11 crates) across balanced courtyard positions.
