Changelog & Patch Notes

Release history, balance tuning, and new tactical features for BATTLE TANK.

VERSION 1.18.2 // SEO CLEAN URLS & GUIDE LAYOUT FIX Clean Internal Links, Local Clean URLs & Compact Guide Cards

Updated all internal navigation and footer links to canonical clean URLs without .html (while preserving .html links in itch.io builds), enabled cleanUrls in public/serve.json for local dev, and fixed scrollable guide page centering, padding, hero banner height, and compact 3-column card sizing.

VERSION 1.18.1 // SCOUT TUNING & TERRAIN POLISH Scout Speed Tuning, Darker Concrete & Grounded Explosive Barrels

Tuned SCOUT base mass (11, total 74) to reduce movement speed by ~10% and smooth hull turning, darkened the CONCRETE block base tint to 0x8c96a3, and removed the square pad under explosive barrels so they sit directly on the underlying ground surface.

VERSION 1.18.0 // SURVIVAL FORTIFICATION & DIRECTIONAL WAVES Chicane HQ Fortress, Clockwise Wave Assaults, Border Spawning & Full HQ Rearm

Upgraded Paris: Last Stand with a double-ring concrete chicane fortress around the Élysée HQ that blocks direct external fire, moved nearby mines to outer approaches, removed in-map spawner pads so enemies enter from outside map bounds, rotated wave assaults clockwise (north → east → south → west) with HUD direction indicators, and enabled Survival HQ shell replenishment up to full ammo capacity at 2× speed.

VERSION 1.17.2 // UNIFIED SEINE STONE BRIDGES Continuous Procedural Stone Bridges in Paris Survival

Replaced per-cell BRIDGE_DECK segments with unified procedural stone bridges (LandmarkModels.createBridge) spanning each rectangular run of bridge cells over the Seine in Paris: Last Stand.

VERSION 1.17.1 // SURVIVAL WAVE BANNER FIX Opaque Background & Bottom Positioning for Next-Wave Banner

Added the missing .bottom-24 and .bg-slate-950/95 CSS rules in index.css and aligned the Survival intermission NEXT: WAVE banner in contract-hud.js with bg-[#0B0F17]/95 so it renders with an opaque backdrop above the bottom HUD without overlapping the top compass.

VERSION 1.17.0 // REINFORCED CONCRETE BREACHES Directional Concrete Wall Damage Models & Exposed Rebar

Added concrete.glb and shared directional breach rendering (breach-models.js, concrete-models.js) so reinforced concrete walls open craters on the specific faces hit by shells and blasts, exposing internal steel rebar.

VERSION 1.16.3 // CLEANER BRICK BREACHES No Stray Slabs Beside Breached Brick Walls

Rebuilt brick.glb so corner and two-face breach states keep only connected masonry; bricks cut off by two craters no longer remain as a loose slab next to the wall.

VERSION 1.16.2 // DIRECTIONAL BRICK BREACHES Multi-Face Brick Damage Models & Updated Brick Asset

Updated brick.glb and brick damage state rendering in brick-models.js, brick-finish.js, terrain-destruction.js, and projectiles.js so brick walls reflect directional, corner, and multi-face shell breaches.

VERSION 1.16.1 // BATTLE HUD LAYOUT & CONTROLS Relocated Hull HP & APS Indicators, Clearer Camera Toggle Button

Moved the HULL HP card to the bottom-left corner next to the tank component damage silhouette, integrated the passive AUTO APS status tile into the bottom module bar alongside RM-1, SMOKE, and ATGM, and replaced the ambiguous top-right camera glyph with a crisp camera SVG icon and [C] preset label.

VERSION 1.16.0 // DETAILED GROUND DECORATIONS World-Space Ground Shader, Procedural Surface Patterns & Campaign Import Map Fix

Upgraded battlefield ground rendering with a world-space GLSL surface finish (multi-scale mineral grain, weathering, and micro-roughness variation) paired with procedural tileable surface patterns for asphalt, concrete, pavers, dirt, gravel, and snow, 2048px high-quality ground textures, and richer decals and road/railway markings. Also added the missing Three.js import map to campaign.html so campaign missions launch cleanly.

VERSION 1.15.0 // PARIS: LAST STAND & CINEMATIC DEFEAT Paris Survival Rework, Textured 3D Landmarks, Turret-Pop Destruction & On-Demand Model Loading

Reworked Survival mode into Paris: Last Stand—a 40×40 fortified central Paris arena with a 120 HP Élysée Palace HQ, timed 60-second waves, 7:00 Victory & Overtime mode, neutral map mines, Seine bridges, and PBR-textured 3D Paris landmarks (Eiffel Tower, Arc de Triomphe, Notre-Dame, Louvre Pyramid, Sacré-Cœur, Concorde Obelisk). Added catastrophic player tank destruction with 3D turret blow-off, secondary cook-off blasts, and a 2.8 s defeat camera delay, fixed HQ projectile/LOS blocking, and implemented per-map lazy loading and unloading of landmark and enemy tank GLB models.

VERSION 1.14.0 // TACTICAL SURFACES & CODEX New Battlefield Surfaces, Water Towers & 3D Codex Fixes

Added five tactical arena surfaces and interactive hazards—ROAD, SWAMP, OIL_SLICK (ignites on fire/blast), CRATER (3D blast funnel with scorched debris), and destructible WATER_TOWER (floods nearby tiles to swamp and extinguishes fires)—along with urban perimeter buildings, campaign/contract/survival map updates, darker weathered wooden crates, and standalone 3D Codex studio fixes.

VERSION 1.13.3 // SENSOR RADAR Player-Centered Tactical Radar

Centered the battlefield sensor radar on the player tank with a 16-cell tactical radius, dynamically positioning HQ, enemy contacts, loot, and contract objectives relative to the player and clamping distant bearings to the outer ring.

VERSION 1.13.2 // ENEMY AGILITY Enemy Hull Turn Rate Tuning

Increased enemy tank hull turn rate by 10% across movement speed tiers for more responsive maneuvering.

VERSION 1.13.1 // UI POLISH & HUD CLARITY Button Redesign & Tank Damage Silhouette Fix

Redesigned menu, modal, share, and drawer-handle buttons with crisp borders and steady glow-ring hover states, centered the contract quick-deploy chevron icon, and updated the battlefield tank silhouette so red highlights persistent component faults requiring paid hangar repair while combat damage displays in yellow.

VERSION 1.13.0 // PWA & BRAND ICONS Web App Manifest & Custom Tank Icon Suite

Added manifest.webmanifest with standalone landscape PWA configuration, a flat-color dark-mode-aware SVG tank icon (icon.svg), and regenerated favicon.ico, apple-touch-icon.png, icon-192.png, icon-512.png, and maskable icon-mask.png.

Auto-selects the Low graphics preset on mobile and tablet devices by default, disables heavy RoomEnvironment IBL and dynamic explosion lights on Low, and reduces HUD/compass DOM rebuild frequency during combat.

Added an on-screen virtual joystick, Fire button, and enlarged combat module buttons for landscape tablets (with Auto/On/Off in Settings), fixed campaign mission URL parameter routing, and streamlined the top-left mission HUD strip.

VERSION 1.11.0 // DISTRIBUTION & HOME UI Home Overview & Distribution Polish

Refined the home hero briefing and overview strip layout, improved desktop/tablet viewport scaling, and added HTML5 distribution support.

VERSION 1.10 // FIELD GUIDES & SEO Walkthrough, Enemy & Arsenal Guides

VERSION 1.9.2 // COMBAT AI Tracked Movement & Flank Reaction Tuning

Capped enemy hull turn rates, aligned tracked movement physics, and added realistic flank reaction delays for smoother tactical engagements.
