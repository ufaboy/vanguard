Campaign Walkthrough & Region Guide

Complete strategic guide to the 35-mission Road to the Capital campaign in BATTLE TANK. Learn how to conquer all 7 tactical regions, prepare your VANGUARD tank upgrades, and defeat the 4 Sector Bosses.

7 Campaign Regions (Missions 01–35)

REGION 01 // MISSIONS 01–05 City Outskirts

Start with First Contact, Broken Avenue, Civic Shelter, Railway Cut, and Outer Gate. Use destructible brick walls for cover, protect your 2-cell Command Base HQ, and invest early credits into Cannon Mk2 and Armor A2.

REGION 02 // MISSIONS 06–10 Forest Frontier

Dense woodland canopies conceal ambushing Flamethrowers, Stealth snipers, and Sapper minelayers. Watch your minimap and upgrade Engine + Chassis to outmaneuver flanking Scouts.

REGION 03 // MISSIONS 11–15 Desert Crossing

Open sightlines favor long-range Howitzers and Striker missile carriers. Culminates in Mission 15 against MAMMOTH MK-V—equip 4-Corner APS and Cannon Mk3+ to break its armor and survive its 50% HP overdrive.

REGION 04 // MISSIONS 16–20 Coastal Wetlands

Deep water channels give amphibious Hover raiders free flanks. Upgrade to CH4 All-Terrain Tracks before facing HYDRA MLRS-9 so you can cross water and dodge telegraphed 3-rocket salvos.

REGION 05 // MISSIONS 21–25 Frozen Highlands

Low-friction ice sheets cause standard tracks to drift, while CH4/CH5 chassis hold crisp traction. Watch out for Engineers repairing concrete chokepoints and heavy Armor spearheads.

REGION 06 // MISSIONS 26–30 Industrial Belt

Heavy 16 HP reinforced concrete corridors require Cannon Mk4 or Mk5 to breach firing lanes. Prepare for combined-arms Jammers, Strikers, and SUPREME DREADNOUGHT (TITAN) with directional Aegis armor.

REGION 07 // MISSIONS 31–35 Capital City

The final push through fortified boulevards to Vanguard Dawn (Mission 35), where an escalating multi-boss gauntlet—including thermobaric leviathan CERBERUS-T—guards the capital.
