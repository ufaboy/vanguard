Tank Arsenal & Modular Upgrades

Your VANGUARD tank in BATTLE TANK is engineered from a base hull (22 HP Tier-1 player hull) plus 5 core upgrade tracks (Tiers I–V) and standalone tactical modules. Press U near your Command Base HQ during combat to open the Garage.

Main Cannon Line (Mk1 – Mk5)

TIER I // STANDARD Cannon Mk1 (I)

20 Damage · 0% Penetration · 1.50s Reload. Reliable baseline tank gun for breaking brick walls and early patrols.

TIER II // LASER RANGEFINDER Cannon Mk2 (II)

22 Damage · 10% Penetration · 1.25s Reload. Higher muzzle velocity (9.5) and faster cycling against light armor.

TIER III // AUTOLOADER Cannon Mk3 (III)

25 Damage · 22% Penetration · 1.20s Reload. Thermal-shrouded barrel that punches through mid-tier reactive plating.

TIER IV & V // SIEGE & TWIN-LINKED Cannon Mk4 (IV) & Mk5 (V)

28 Damage · 32% Penetration · 1.00s Reload. Heavy siege power that shatters 16 HP reinforced concrete. Mk5 fires a rapid twin-barrel double salvo.

Chassis, Frame, Engine & Armor Tracks

MOBILITY // CH1 – CH5 Chassis & All-Terrain Crossings

Progresses from 5-roadwheel steel tracks (CH1) and hydro-shock treads (CH2–CH3) to CH4 All-Terrain Tracks and CH5 Grav-Hover—both cross deep water and drive over ice, mud, and sand with zero slip or slowdown.

STRUCTURE // F1 – F5 Hull Frame & Magazine Capacity

Reinforces the hull from welded monocoque (F1) and bull-bar (F2) to composite exo-truss (F4) and Titan siege prow (F5), scaling cannon ammo capacity from 15 to 50 rounds as well as APS and ATGM magazine limits.

PROPULSION // E1 – E5 Engines & Power-to-Weight

Speed depends directly on total tank mass versus engine horsepower—from diesel louvers (E1), V8 turbo (E2), and V12 twin-intercooler (E3) up to a gas-turbine nacelle (E4) and twin-core fusion reactor (E5).

PROTECTION // A1 – A5 Directional Armor & Penetration

Evolves from rolled steel (A1) and appliqué skirts (A2) to Kontakt-5 ERA (A3), Chobham cheeks (A4), and Relikt citadel armor (A5). Effective Armor = Armor × (1 − Penetration / 100), evaluated per hit side (front, left, right, rear).

Standalone Modules & Field Power-Ups

DEFENSE MODULE // APS_MODULE 4-Corner Active Protection System

Intercepts Cannon Mk1–Mk3 shells and all missiles for 1 charge each; heavy Cannon Mk4/Mk5, Howitzer, and Mammoth Dual shells cost 2 charges. Cannot intercept Flamethrower streams or Titan Rail slugs.

OFFENSE & SURVIVAL MODULES Homing ATGM Pod & Auto-Repair Kit

Homing ATGM Pod: Launch guided anti-tank missiles with Space or Right Click.
Auto-Repair Kit: Automatically restores +20 HP when hull integrity drops below 33%.

BATTLEFIELD DROPS // BOX CACHES 5 Tactical Field Power-Ups

Break wooden BOX caches to roll Repair, EMP (stuns hostiles), Base Restore (rebuilds destroyed HQ), APS charges, or Missile reloads—plus cannon ammo drops.

HANGAR LOGISTICS Resupply & Component Repairs

Refill APS charges (1,000 credits) or ATGM missiles (3,000 credits) in the Hangar, and repair battle-damaged Tier II–V components (600 / 900 / 1,200 / 1,500 credits by tier).
