1. Choose Your Operation

Start in Campaign with First Contact (20 hostiles), then break through Broken Avenue, Civic Shelter, and Railway Cut. On defense missions, protect the allied Command Base HQ—losing either HQ cell ends the operation. Test endless waves in Survival or run high-stakes Contracts for extra credits.

2. Fight Smart & Flank

Read the 36×36 arena: smash brick walls to open firing lanes, watch out for 16 HP reinforced concrete, and use forest canopies for concealment. Enemy armor is directional—flank heavy tanks and bosses like Titan from the sides or rear instead of trading shots into their glacis.

3. Upgrade in the Hangar

Spend earned credits in the Hangar & Workshop (press U near your base during a mission) to upgrade Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine, and Armor from Tier I to Tier V, and equip standalone modules like 4-Corner APS, Homing ATGM pods, and Auto-Repair Kits.

Tactical Mechanics & Q&A

How do tank stats and armor penetration work?

Every tank combines a base hull with 5 core tracks (Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine, Armor) and optional modules. Armor is evaluated per hit side (front, left, right, rear). Cannon penetration reduces the target’s effective armor percentage before final damage is applied.

How do I cross water or drive smoothly on ice?

Upgrade your Chassis to Tier IV (All-Terrain Tracks) or Tier V (Grav-Hover). Both cross deep water and handle ice, mud, and sand without slip or slowdown.

How does the Active Protection System (APS) work?

The 4-corner APS intercepts incoming Cannon Mk1–Mk3 shells and missiles for 1 charge each. Heavy Cannon Mk4/Mk5, Howitzer, and Mammoth Dual shells cost 2 charges. Flamethrowers and Titan Rail shots bypass APS.

How do BOX supply drops and field repairs work?

Shoot wooden BOX caches to roll tactical Power-Ups (Repair, EMP, Base Restore, APS, or Missile). Your Command Base HQ also repairs your hull while it remains intact. Below 33% hull integrity, movement speed drops by 30%.

How are credits, repairs, and progress saved?

Campaign unlocks, credits, component tiers, and survival records are stored locally in your browser via localStorage and persist across sessions.

What happens if I abort a mission early?

Collected credits and salvaged loot remain in your save, while uncollected battlefield drops are lost.
