About Iron Vanguard

IRON VANGUARD is a 3D isometric tactical tank assault game inspired by classic Battle City, engineered with modular armored warfare and destructible 36×36 arenas.

DESTRUCTIBLE ARENAS 36 × 36 Tactical Grids

Every sector spans 1,296 cells with multi-block destructible brick, reinforced 16 HP concrete, stealth forest canopies, water crossings, low-friction ice, and fortified Command Bases.

MODULAR ENGINEERING 5 Tracks × 5 Tiers

Upgrade Cannon, Chassis, Frame, Engine, and Armor from Tier I to Tier V with live 3D visual changes, plus standalone modules like 4-Corner APS, Homing ATGM pods, and Auto-Repair Kits.

TACTICAL AI & BOSSES 12 Enemy Classes & 3 Bosses

Face flanking Scouts, indirect-fire Howitzers, stealth Snipers, Sapper minelayers, Flamethrowers, and three unique Sector Bosses: Mammoth, Hydra, and the directional-shield Titan.

PURE WEB STACK Zero-Bundler Three.js

Built strictly with native ES Modules, HTML5, standalone CSS, and Three.js WebGL — designed for instant browser launch with hardware or software WebGL fallback.
