Destructible Terrain & 40×40 Tactical Arenas

BATTLE TANK is a free 3D isometric tank game where the map is part of the fight. Every battle takes place on a 40×40 grid of 1,600 cells, and almost every wall, crate and building can be shot apart. This guide covers each terrain type, how much punishment it takes, and how to use it for cover, ambushes and shortcuts.

Destructible Walls: Brick, Concrete and Buildings

Walls are the core of every arena. You can hide behind them, shoot through them to open a new lane, or leave them standing to protect your Command Base HQ. How quickly a wall falls depends on your cannon: Mk1–Mk3 shells chip walls slowly, while the heavy Mk4 and Mk5 guns tear them down several times faster.

BRICK // 4 HP Destructible Brick Wall

Each cell is built from 4 sub-blocks. Mk1–Mk3 shells remove one layer per hit; Mk4/Mk5 heavy shells remove two. Enemy Engineer tanks can rebuild brick walls during the fight.

CONCRETE // 16 HP Reinforced Concrete

A hard wall with a steel rebar cage. Standard shells deal 1 HP per hit (16 hits), Mk4/Mk5 shells deal 4 HP and artillery blasts 6 HP. Craters open on the faces that were hit and expose the rebar.

BUILDINGS // 25–70 HP Houses, Factories & Offices

One- and two-story buildings with 5 to 7 visible damage stages. Shells strip the facade and roof down to the frame, then the building collapses into rubble that slows every tank.

FORTRESS // 48–100 HP Enemy Strongholds & Turrets

In stronghold assault missions the enemy citadel (48 HP) is ringed by concrete and guarded by cannon, howitzer and rocket turrets (100 HP each). Destroying the citadel wins the mission.

Cover, Concealment and Line of Sight

Directional armor makes positioning decisive: tanks are strongest from the front and weakest from the rear, so terrain that hides your flank is worth as much as a cannon upgrade. Enemies use the same rules, and their AI actively looks for forest, chokepoints and cells hidden from you.

FOREST Camouflage Canopy

Tanks inside forest cells are hidden from view and break the lock of enemy guided missiles. Flamethrower tanks and fuel-air blasts can burn a forest down to bare scorched sand.

HEDGEHOGS Anti-Tank Obstacles

Steel hedgehogs block every chassis but sit low enough for shells and missiles to fly over them, which makes them ideal firing positions at chokepoints.

CRATERS Blast Craters

Heavy explosions gouge craters into the ground. A tank sitting in a crater takes 30% less splash and artillery damage, at the cost of a 15% speed penalty.

LANDMARKS Monuments

Indestructible landmarks block movement, shells, missiles and line of sight completely. They are permanent cover that no weapon can remove.

Water, Ice, Mud and Other Surfaces

Every cell has a ground surface that changes how tanks drive. Speed, acceleration and turning are combined from the surface and anything standing on it, so a crater on a road keeps part of the road bonus. The CH4 All-Terrain and CH5 Grav-Hover chassis ignore almost all of these penalties.

WATER Deep Water Channels

Standard tracks (CH1–CH3) cannot enter deep water, but shells and missiles fly across it freely. The CH4 All-Terrain chassis wades across and the CH5 Grav-Hover glides over it, opening routes enemies do not expect.

ICE Low-Traction Ice

Acceleration drops from 22 to 4.5 and braking from 14 to 1.8, so heavy tanks slide through turns. Plan your stops early or fit an all-terrain chassis.

SWAMP & SAND Mud and Scorched Sand

Swamp slows tracked tanks to 58% speed and 65% turn rate. Sand left behind by burned forest costs 22% speed and offers no concealment.

ROADS & STONE Highways, Bridges and Rock Fields

Roads and bridges give +20% top speed and +25% acceleration but no cover. Rock fields cap tracked tanks at 1.5 cells per second, whatever the engine.

Explosive and Interactive Objects

Some objects react to fire and can change a whole section of the map in a second. Learning them turns the arena into a weapon.

FUEL DRUMS Explosive Barrels

Any hit detonates a drum for 35 damage in a 2.8-cell radius. The blast knocks down nearby brick, damages concrete and sets off neighbouring drums in a chain reaction.

OIL SLICKS Volatile Oil

Oil makes tracks drift like ice. A shell, flame or nearby explosion ignites it for 7 seconds of fire damage and thick smoke that hides tanks.

WATER TOWERS Pressurized Water Towers

Shooting a tower (2 HP) floods the ground around it into swamp and puts out every forest and oil fire within 2.5 cells.

SUPPLY BOXES Wooden Supply Caches

Two hits open a cache and drop a field power-up (repair, EMP, APS charge, missile, cannon shells or HQ restore) that waits until you drive over it.

Defending the Command Base HQ

Your Command Base is a two-bay workshop on the edge of the map. A 4-cell line of reinforced concrete stands two rows in front of it, with an open lane so you can drive up for repairs (+3 HP per second within 2.5 cells). Every enemy that breaks through that line threatens the HQ, so many players keep the concrete intact and fight in front of it rather than behind it.
